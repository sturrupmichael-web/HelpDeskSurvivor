Privacy policy

Helpdesk Survivors · Last updated October 20, 2026

Helpdesk Survivors is a free browser game made by Michael Sturrup and played at game.michaelsturrup.com. This page explains what the game stores about you and why. The short version: as little as possible, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold.

Stored on your device

Your progress (Overtime Pay, unlocks, records, achievements, the Help Desk Wiki) and your settings are kept in your browser's storage. They stay on your device unless you use an account (below). Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

The leaderboard

If you post a score, the leaderboard stores your leaderboard name, the map, your character, how long you lasted, your CSAT score, whether you survived the shift and when you posted it. Everything on the leaderboard is public.

Accounts (optional)

You never need an account to play. If you post a score or sign in, an account is created so your leaderboard name stays yours:

  • Guest account: a random account ID. No personal information.
  • Sign in with Google: your email address and the basic profile details Google shares (your name and profile picture link). We use your email only to show which account you're signed in with and to link your account to Google. We never email you.
  • Cloud save: a copy of your game progress, so it can follow you to other devices. Only you can read or change it.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the leaderboard, accounts and cloud saves.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google" if you choose it.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game. Like any website host, it may briefly log technical details such as your IP address to keep the site running.

The game has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, cloud save or leaderboard scores, contact us (below) with your leaderboard name and, if you used Google, the email address you signed in with. We'll delete them within 30 days. Guest accounts that are never used can be removed at any time.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone, but accounts are not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
